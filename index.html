› Jacketed / Packed-Bed Reactor Scale-Up Calculator lab → pilot

Scales a jacketed or packed-bed PFR exothermic reaction from lab to pilot diameter, holding target conversion and L/D fixed while sweeping tube diameter D. Every reaction has its own binding scale-up constraint — cooling surface/volume, thermal stability, or pressure drop — derived from first principles and verified public literature (see reference panel), not vendor/proprietary numbers. Pick a reaction and L/D; every result and chart recomputes instantly from a precomputed sweep (Python/SciPy, baked in at build time — no live solver in the browser).
